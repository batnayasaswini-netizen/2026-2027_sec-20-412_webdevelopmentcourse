Welcome to Our Tourism Website

India is a beautiful country with many historical monuments, famous tourist destinations, colourful festivals and delicious food. This website provides information about some popular tourist places and the culture of India.

Tourism helps us learn about history, traditions and the beauty of different places.

Famous Tourist Places

Taj Mahal

Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh

The Taj Mahal is a famous historical monument Built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

Jaipur

Location: Rajasthan

Jaipur is known for its beautiful palaces, forts and traditional culture.

Indian Heritage

Location: Different Regions of India

India has many ancient temples, monuments and historical buildings.

Famous Indian Festivals

  • Diwali: The Festival of Lights celebrated throughout India.
  • Holi: The festival of colours and happiness.
  • Pongal: A harvest festival celebrated mainly in Tamil Nadu.
  • Durga Puja: A popular festival celebrated especially in West Bengal.
  • Ugadi: The traditional New Year festival in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Famous Indian Food

Biryani

A popular rice dish (prepared with spices and either vegetables or meat).

Dosa

A South Indian dish, served with chutney and sambar.

Gulab Jamun

A traditional Indian sweet, eaten at festivals.

Travel Tips

  • Plan your trip prior to travelling.
  • Take your ID and all the necessary docs.
  • Keep the tourist sites clean.
  • Show respect for local culture and traditions.
  • Check the weather prior to setting off.
  • Keep your belongings safe.
